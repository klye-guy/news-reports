## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Java is today's patch priority. On 17 NOV Oracle and the OpenJDK project fixed "Java4Shell" (CVE-2026-114938), a memory-corruption bug in the HotSpot JVM's x86-64 Base64 decoder. CISA issued a "Critical Java Vulnerability Requires Immediate Patching" alert that evening. The bug only triggers on CPUs with AVX-512 VBMI (Intel Ice Lake or later, AMD Zen 4 or later); ARM64 is not affected. Crashing a JVM is trivial. Calif, which found the bug, says it achieved unauthenticated RCE in default Keycloak, Jira, Confluence, Artifactory, Jenkins and PingFederate. No exploitation has been reported, and VulnCheck and watchTowr do not expect it to be indiscriminate. Update every JDK, including the runtimes bundled inside appliances and containers, or set `-XX:+UnlockDiagnosticVMOptions -XX:-UseBASE64Intrinsics` until you can. Second, CISA added Perforce Puppet Enterprise CVE-2026-115731 to KEV (due 01 DEC), the only KEV addition of the period (1,782 entries). Any agent certificate, or any attacker at all where autosigning is loose, can run code on the Puppet server and reach its CA key. Third, a credential stealer shipped in Microsoft's official `agent-framework` PyPI package (versions 1.26.1 and 1.26.2) for about 45 minutes on Tuesday afternoon. It targets Azure managed-identity, Azure DevOps and GitHub Actions tokens. Correction: Debian's kernel fix is not "the same 6.1.0-55 package." linux 6.1.192-2 ships as **linux-image-6.1.0-56-amd64**, so `uname -r` should read 6.1.0-56 after the reboot. The outage run has an official, partial explanation. DNI Clayton tied the NSA outage to patching after the PHP2Shell flaws, Apple blamed an internal update, and the FAA paused Juniper installs. A Washington Sun report that AI-driven patch rushes caused the outages broadly rests on anonymous sources. Codeberg went dark for hours after its registrar, NameSilo, suspended codeberg.org. Microsoft's ONNX Runtime repo was still returning 404 at report time. In big tech, Nvidia's interim CEO said it is "no longer shipping" licensed Hopper data-center chips to China, and its board chair said Huang will not be reinstated. Microsoft's Ignite put Office inside Copilot and previewed Windows Server 2028 with hardened defaults. Ars reported that Grok Bot, the agent producing this report, ran unprompted connector check-ins for one company; a "proactive messages" admin toggle now exists. Also in the period: Apache Roller is being exploited, Equinor is fighting a cyberattack, and a Russian group, Cold Relic, is chaining Workspace-to-Entra intrusions. ## ITEM 7: Java4Shell (CVE-2026-114938) — OpenJDK Base64 Intrinsic Heap Overflow, Pre-Auth RCE Shown in Keycloak, Jira, Confluence, Jenkins; AVX-512 VBMI CPUs Only; CISA, CERT-EU, Canada Say Patch Now

- WHAT / WHERE / WHEN: Oracle and the OpenJDK project shipped fixes on 17 NOV. CISA's alert followed that evening: it "strongly urges organizations to immediately patch" and tells defenders to find JDKs "bundled with third-party applications or included in container images and appliances." The flaw is a heap-based buffer overflow (Canadian Cyber Centre AL26-029) in the AVX-512 Base64 decode intrinsic. Calif: "The corruption itself can be triggered by just 64 A characters followed by a single =" (128 on older JDK 17). Conditions (CERT-EU): x86-64 with avx512f, avx512bw, avx512vbmi and bmi2 (Intel Xeon Ice Lake or later, AMD Zen 4 or later), the intrinsic active (the default), and attacker-controlled Base64 reaching `java.util.Base64.Decoder`, for example via HTTP Basic auth, JWTs, SAML, cookies or MIME. ARM64 is not affected. Calif reports unauthenticated RCE in default Keycloak, Jira, Confluence, JFrog Artifactory, Jenkins and PingFederate, and authenticated RCE in Kafka, Solr, Elasticsearch, Gerrit and Spinnaker. Oracle scores it CVSS 3.1 8.1 (high complexity); Calif scores it CVSS 4.0 9.2. VulnCheck: "Crashing an affected JVM is trivial," but RCE needs heap grooming plus a separate memory leak to bypass ASLR. Calif says it used AI and found the bug in an hour, wrote the first exploit in two more, and spent under $200 in tokens. It has published only a reproduction checker and is withholding product exploit chains. No in-the-wild exploitation is reported (Help Net Security; Cyber Centre: "not aware of active exploitation"). Fixed Oracle builds: 8u513 (perf builds), 11.0.33.1, 17.0.21.1, 21.0.13.1, 25.0.5.1, 27.0.1.1 (Tenable). CERT-EU says OpenJDK 17 is affected from 17.0.6. Distribution builds from Corretto, Temurin, Microsoft, Zulu, SapMachine, Liberica and Red Hat are rolling out. The same Oracle Java update fixes CVE-2026-114939 (JGSS) and CVE-2026-86144 (JavaFX libxml2).
- IMPACT ASSESSMENT: The reach is enormous, but exploitation is gated by the CPU. Most modern Xeon and EPYC cloud hosts qualify; Graviton and other ARM hosts do not. The realistic near-term risk is denial of service against any internet-facing Java login or SSO endpoint, with targeted RCE against identity products (Keycloak, PingFederate) and dev tooling (Jenkins, Artifactory). Bundled JREs inside vendor appliances will lag.
- ACTION FOR ADMINS: Inventory JVMs (`java -version`, container base images, appliance release notes) and check CPU flags with `lscpu | grep -o 'avx512vbmi'`. Patch internet-facing Keycloak, PingFederate, Jira/Confluence, Jenkins and Artifactory first, and restart every JVM. Where you can't patch today, add `-XX:+UnlockDiagnosticVMOptions -XX:-UseBASE64Intrinsics` (or `-XX:UseAVX=2`, slower), restart, and verify with `java -XX:+PrintFlagsFinal -version | grep UseBASE64Intrinsics`. Alert on unexpected JVM crashes and hs_err files.
- SOURCE SYNOPSIS: CISA alert (read directly); The Record and Help Net Security (read directly); CERT-EU, Canadian Cyber Centre, CSA Singapore, Tenable (search excerpts); The Hacker News and BleepingComputer (headlines); calif.io advisory (via trade quotes, not read in full).
- FRAMING (TRADE/MAINSTREAM): The Record: "an AI-found Java bug touching most of the Fortune 500." Help Net: "Exploitation is tricky and hinges on specific CPUs." Kevin Beaumont: "not world ending. Just patch as you normally do."
- FRAMING (COMMUNITY/ALT: HN, Reddit, X, forums): HN's calif.io thread drew 1,031 points and 273 comments; a second thread on Aikido's write-up drew 149 points. The X news summary says Calif used OpenAI's Codex (not verified) and highlights one researcher's blunt "If you're running a networked Java application turn it off," mixed with sysadmin relief at a clear warning.

## ITEM 8: KEV — Perforce Puppet Enterprise CVE-2026-115731 Added (Due 01 DEC): Report Deserialization Gives Code Execution on the Puppet Server and Its CA Key; Autosigning Makes It Unauthenticated

- WHAT / WHERE / WHEN: CISA added CVE-2026-115731 on 17 NOV (catalog 2026.11.17, released 1153 CST; 1,782 entries; due 01 DEC; ransomware use "Unknown"). A client with any CA-signed agent certificate can submit a report with a crafted rich-data type by PUT and execute code on Puppet Server as the service user. If the CA "automatically signs requests without strong verification, an unauthenticated network attacker can obtain a valid certificate" (KEV entry). Perforce: "A successful attack could expose the server's certificate-signing key, allow an attacker to issue trusted certificates, and enable changes to software and configuration delivered to managed systems on their next Puppet run." Fixes shipped 03 NOV alongside CVE-2026-115732, a certificate-name impersonation flaw: Puppet Enterprise 2026.0.1, 2025.12.1 and 2023.8.13, and Puppet Core 9.1.3 and 8.22.2. SecurityWeek says the flaw was flagged as exploited on 04 NOV. A PoC targeting OpenVox was published shortly after the patch, and OpenVox Server is fixed in 9.2.0 and 8.18.0. CVSS: 9.4 (v4, Puppet) and 9.9 (v3.1, NVD). Red Hat Satellite processes client reports by default; follow Red Hat's guidance (cyber-threat.net summary). Neodyme, which found the bug, traces it back to open-source Puppet 6.24.0 (2021).
- IMPACT ASSESSMENT: A Puppet server is a fleet-wide root of trust. Compromise means attacker code on every managed node at the next run. Environments that autosign for Satellite or Ansible provisioning are exposed to unauthenticated attack.
- ACTION FOR ADMINS: Patch to the fixed PE, Core or OpenVox versions. Turn off basic autosigning, or require policy-based verification. Restrict port 8140 to agent networks. Review recently signed certificates and look for unusual child processes of puppetserver. If the server was exposed, treat the CA as possibly compromised and follow CISA's forensics-triage guidance.
- SOURCE SYNOPSIS: CISA KEV JSON and alert (read directly); SecurityWeek, cyber-threat.net, Stackurity, CIRCL (search excerpts); The Hacker News (headline).

## ITEM 11: Other Exploitation and Research — Apache Roller CVE-2026-129448 Exploited (Pre-Auth XML-RPC RCE, Fix 6.1.6+); TU Berlin Resets fTPM Lockouts to Brute-Force Hello/BitLocker PINs, AMD Fixes Through FEB 2027; N-central and ScreenConnect Unchanged

- WHAT / WHERE / WHEN: Apache Roller: Apache's Tuesday advisory says CVE-2026-129448 lets unauthenticated attackers execute code on 4.0.0 through 6.1.4 because "the XML-RPC endpoint accepts vendor extension types and deserializes them before authenticating the requester." It is "the same vulnerability as CVE-2026-82384," which had listed only 6.1.5, and "is being actively exploited." Fix: 6.1.6 or later. Mitigation: set `enabledForExtensions` to false in WEB-INF/web.xml or remove the `/roller-services/xmlrpc` mapping, then restart. Toggling XML-RPC off in the admin UI is not enough (BleepingComputer, 0435 CST 18 NOV). It is not in KEV yet. TPM lockout bypass: TU Berlin researchers reset firmware-TPM anti-hammering state by replaying the TPM's saved state on the flash chip. On AMD, firmware never verifies that the RPMC counter increased. On Winbond RPMC flash, a voltage glitch leaks the counter-signing key. Physical access with the case open is required. In the lab they found a 4-digit Hello PIN on an HP OmniBook 3 (Ryzen AI 5 330) in 57 minutes and a 6-digit BitLocker PIN on a Framework 13 (Core Ultra 5 125H) in 6 minutes 55 seconds. Their worst-case estimates: 40 hours (4-digit Hello) and 20 days (6-digit BitLocker). AMD confirmed the flaw on 17 NOV, with fixes for most affected chips between November and February 2027. Intel says no Intel component is vulnerable, and Winbond has not committed to a fix. Microsoft rated the Hello report "moderate," issued no CVE, and committed to a six-digit minimum Hello PIN (The Hacker News, 0628 CST 18 NOV). MSP tools: no new N-central or ScreenConnect exploitation was reported in the period. Yesterday's guidance stands: HF3 (2026.4.1.39) on servers, Agents and Probes; ScreenConnect host clients 26.6.5+.
- IMPACT ASSESSMENT: Roller is niche, but internet-facing instances are being hit now. The TPM work matters for stolen or lost laptops. Short PINs on AMD fTPM machines are no longer protected by lockout against a hands-on attacker.
- ACTION FOR ADMINS: Upgrade or mitigate any Roller instance today. For Windows fleets, raise the Hello for Business minimum PIN length and BitLocker's "Configure minimum PIN length for startup" to 8 digits or more, or enable enhanced PINs. Apply AMD firmware updates through your OEM as they ship.
- SOURCE SYNOPSIS: BleepingComputer (read directly); Corgea and VulDB (search excerpts); The Hacker News (read directly).
- FRAMING (TRADE/MAINSTREAM): THN: "Crack Windows Hello and BitLocker PINs in Days, Not Years."
- FRAMING (COMMUNITY/ALT: HN, Reddit, X, forums): No major community threads found for either item.
- CONFIDENCE: HIGH on Roller (Apache advisory as relayed). HIGH on the TPM paper's lab results; the researchers' wider claims are untested.
