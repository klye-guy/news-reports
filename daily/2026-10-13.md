AP (two regional officials, 12 OCT): Saudi Arabia opened an inquiry into why air defenses failed; initial finding is that drones were launched first to clear a path for cruise and ballistic missiles flying over 1,000 km. Strikes on Saudi airports and Aramco sites add to oil risk premium (Monday's oil gain was attributed partly to these attacks) and threaten Red Sea/Bab al-Mandab routing as an alternative to Hormuz.
