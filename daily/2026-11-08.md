## ITEM 2: Iran — CENTCOM Launches Task Force Iron Gate; Qatar Plan Details Emerge; Iran Reviewing; $20M Nuclear Reward; New Blasts on Qeshm and Jask

- WHAT / WHERE / WHEN: On Saturday, 07 NOV, CENTCOM said Adm. Brad Cooper directed formation of Task Force Iron Gate, about 20 civilian and military personnel in Tampa, to coordinate with other agencies on preventing "the movement of advanced weapons systems, dual-use technologies, sanctioned materials, and illegal revenue streams across Iran's land borders and the Caspian Sea" (CENTCOM via Fox News; Washington Post). The Guardian reported Qatar's proposal: the US would immediately lift the naval blockade and release a portion of about $8 billion in frozen Iranian assets; Iran would allow IAEA visits to bombed nuclear sites based on the abandoned September 2025 Cairo agreement, without committing to remove its enriched-uranium stockpile (down-blending is an option), and would reopen Hormuz on day two via a route negotiated with Oman. Iran's other preconditions include ending the war in Lebanon, US forces' withdrawal from Iran's coastline, some sanctions relief, and reparations (Guardian). AP, citing two officials, reported Iran is reviewing Qatar's proposals but remains unwilling to negotiate on nuclear issues until the US meets several conditions (via ABC Australia). State's Rewards for Justice offered up to $20 million for information on Iran's nuclear personnel and programs, and State reissued its Middle East security alert citing Houthi attack threats and Iranian efforts to target US officials (ABC News). Iranian state media reported two explosions on Qeshm and several in the port of Jask Saturday evening, possibly tied to Iranian military activity; no US strike was confirmed (ABC Australia/AP/Reuters). Trump said there has been "progress" on a deal (ABC News).
- US IMPACT ASSESSMENT: Iron Gate widens the pressure campaign from sea to land and Caspian routes, signaling Washington is tightening rather than easing while diplomacy runs. Qatar's sequencing (blockade relief first) is the main path to reopening Hormuz and lowering US fuel costs, but Iran's added preconditions keep a deal distant.
- SOURCE SYNOPSIS: CENTCOM statement via Fox News live page and Washington Post (07 NOV); The Guardian (07 NOV, read directly); ABC News live updates via Yahoo (08 NOV, read directly); ABC Australia compiling AP/Reuters (08 NOV, read directly); LA Times/AP (07 NOV, search).
- MEDIA FRAMING (RIGHT): Fox News emphasized the $20 million reward, Iron Gate, and Trump's statement that Iran is eager for a deal because of economic troubles and military damage.
- MEDIA FRAMING (LEFT/MAINSTREAM): The Guardian said Trump's threats are "less credible" inside Iran after Vance tied the war to the midterm losses and described Tehran as "triumphalist"; AP framed the review as raising hopes of indirect nuclear talks.
- CONFIDENCE: HIGH on Iron Gate, reward, and security alert (official statements). MODERATE on Qatar plan details (Guardian, unofficial). LOW on the cause of the Qeshm/Jask explosions.
