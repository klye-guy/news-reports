## ITEM 1: Blockade Enforcement — CENTCOM Disables Palau-Flagged Bulker Pacific Archer in the Gulf of Oman (26 NOV), Tally Now 158 Redirected / Six Disabled; Seatrade/Pole Star AIS Shows No Iranian Port Call, Contradicting CENTCOM's "Departure From Bandar Abbas"; UKMTO Advisory 224-26 Logs the Incident; CENTCOM Rebuts Iranian Navy Chief's Tanker-Recapture, Abraham Lincoln and $2.5M-Toll Claims: "Iran No Longer Has a Navy"

- WHAT / WHERE / WHEN: CENTCOM (DVIDS release dated 27 NOV; X post 1055 CST 27 NOV): "U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) disabled a commercial vessel in the Gulf of Oman, Nov. 26, after the crew attempted to run the U.S. naval blockade, despite multiple warnings, following departure from an Iranian port. A U.S. military aircraft launched precision munitions that struck the stern of Palau-flagged M/V Pacific Archer. The ship's propulsion was ultimately disabled and the civilian crew was unharmed. The bulk carrier attempted to transit regional waters after departing Bandar Abbas." CENTCOM says US forces "have redirected 158 compliant commercial vessels and disabled six that refused to comply." AP adds that ship-tracking sites list the vessel's destination as Singapore. Seatrade Maritime (Marcus Hand, 28 NOV), citing Pole Star Global AIS data, reports a different track. The bulker sailed from anchorage near Al Fujayrah on 23 NOV and reached the approximate blockade line early on 25 NOV (UTC). It turned toward Iran, then turned back toward the UAE about nine hours later. It was disabled on 26 NOV about 30 km from Khor Fakkan. Seatrade says that "AIS data from the previous months show no sign that the Pacific Archer had called the port of Bandar Abbas or any Iranian port." Its previous calls were Al Jubayl (Saudi Arabia) and Umm Qasr (Iraq), and it is not subject to any international sanctions program. OSINT accounts on X (Live-AIS.com, MenchOsint) made similar AIS arguments, and one says CENTCOM's video shows the ship at anchor; these accounts are leads only. AIS can be spoofed or switched off, so neither account is conclusive. UKMTO Advisory 224-26 (issued 1415 CST 27 NOV; read directly) reports "an incident involving a cargo vessel and military forces in the Gulf of Oman" on 26 NOV, time TBC, source "Military authorities." It is assessed, not confirmed, to be the Pacific Archer action. Rebuttal: At 1805 CST 27 NOV, CENTCOM posted on X that "An Iranian military commander made multiple FALSE claims concerning U.S. Forces over the past 24 hours." Answering Rear Adm. Shahram Irani's claim that Iranian commandos retook two US-seized tankers while US troops slept, CENTCOM wrote: "Iran no longer has a navy. The entire situation the Iranian military commander described is another fabricated lie." It said the Abraham Lincoln strike group "was well defended and never struck by Iran" and returned safely to the US last month. It said that while US forces and partners control the waterway, "there is no toll," and that "thousands of ships and billions of crude oil barrels have transited the strait freely and safely under US protection" (Anadolu; Al Arabiya; Arab News; Middle East Eye). Trump (Jerusalem Post/Reuters): "Not one Iranian ship has gotten through the blockade since we established it," except for "a little moratorium."
- US IMPACT ASSESSMENT: This is the sixth disabling strike and the first since the 11 NOV "nearly 150 redirected / five disabled" figures carried in earlier dailies. The tempo rose alongside Iran's renewed tanker attacks. The AIS dispute matters. If the ship had no Iranian port call, a US strike on a non-sanctioned, non-Iranian-origin bulker heading out of the Gulf invites legal and diplomatic challenge and gives Tehran and Beijing a talking point. Several OSINT posters describe the ship as Chinese-owned; that is unverified. CENTCOM has not published track evidence. Its blunt rebuttal of Irani shows the information fight running alongside the kinetic one.
- SOURCE SYNOPSIS: CENTCOM release (DVIDS) and X post (official); AP; CBS live blog; Seatrade Maritime with Pole Star AIS; UKMTO 224-26 PDF (read directly); Anadolu, Al Arabiya, Arab News and MEE on the CENTCOM rebuttal; Jerusalem Post/Reuters for Trump's quote; X OSINT posts (leads only).
