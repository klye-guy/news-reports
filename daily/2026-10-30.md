## ITEM 1: Hormuz — Limpet Mines Disable Tanker Louise 1 Northeast of Khasab; UKMTO 186-26 Projectile Report; Iran Claims Four Mined Tankers and Two More "Halted"; CENTCOM Denies Mine Claims

- WHAT / WHERE / WHEN: UKMTO Warning 186-26 said a company security officer reported a tanker struck by an unknown projectile at about 1600 GMT on 29 OCT while completing an outbound transit of the Strait of Hormuz, with no crew injuries or environmental impact (Anadolu; Gulf News). In an advisory note on 30 OCT, UKMTO said a small boat attached two explosive devices to a tanker's hull 25 NM northeast of Khasab, Oman, on 29 OCT, causing an engine-room fire that temporarily disabled it; the fire was out, the crew safe, and the vessel continuing to its next port (Reuters via Arab News). Three maritime security sources told Reuters the ship was the Panama-flagged Louise 1, hit with limpet mines, the first successful use of such devices in the strait since the war began in February, after a failed attempt in September. It was not clear who carried out the attack. Whether the 186-26 report and the Louise 1 advisory describe the same incident was not confirmed in the sources reviewed; CBS says the projectile strike was on a ship exiting by the US-promoted southern route. On 29 OCT the IRGC said three tankers caught fire after hitting mines on the "unauthorized southern route" near the Omani coast, and Iran's army said a fourth burned east of the strait (CNBC). On 30 OCT the IRGC said two more "offending oil tankers" on the unauthorized route "encountered incidents and were brought to a halt" south of the strait, saying they had switched off navigation systems "with the support of the terrorist US military" (AFP via Arab News; CBS). CENTCOM said on 28 OCT that Iranian claims of ships hitting mines "are not true" and that US forces have cleared all main internationally recognized transit routes (CNN Arabic). IRGC spokesman Hossein Mohebi replied: "Prove the claim that the Strait of Hormuz has been cleared of mines with independent evidence, not a statement" (CNBC). Yesterday's UKMTO report of a burning vessel 9 NM north of Khasab at 0300 GMT on 29 OCT is still unexplained.
- US IMPACT ASSESSMENT: A small boat placing limpet mines on a tanker in the US-promoted lane is a new and cheap threat that mine clearance does not address, so it undercuts the US message that the southern route is safe even if Iran's sea-mine claims are false. Expect higher insurance and freight costs and more pressure on US escorts. It also adds to the case for action that Cooper briefed this week.
