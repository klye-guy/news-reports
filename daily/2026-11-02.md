## ITEM 1: Iran Diplomacy — Iranian Team Leaves Doha; Draft Proposal Circulating; Tehran Awaits US Reply on Two-Day Hormuz Plan

- WHAT / WHERE / WHEN: Iranian negotiators left Doha on Sunday, 01 NOV, after a third day of talks with Qatari mediators, "though not because of difficulties in the negotiations," and "a draft proposal is being circulated with a follow-up meeting possible," an official briefed on the talks told Reuters on 02 NOV (Reuters via Al-Monitor). Two Iranian officials told AP that messages with the US continue through the mediator and that Tehran is waiting for the latest US response to a proposal to fully reopen the Strait of Hormuz within two days if Washington meets several conditions (AP). Foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said the basis of the talks is the seven-step plan Iran drew up in September, now on a faster timeline, and that Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi was in Doha to pass messages to Washington. He said: "No final agreement can be initiated, nor can the Strait of Hormuz be reopened or secured, unless the US naval blockade is brought to an end, Iran's demands are respected, and Iran's legitimate requirements are addressed." Asked about the midterms, he said Tehran does not expect them to "give birth to a new US" (Reuters via Al-Monitor). Foreign Minister Araghchi told state media on 01 NOV that talks on the "two-day proposal" are "ongoing and progressing" but have reached no final conclusion, and that Iran is ready for "both defence and diplomacy" (Reuters via CNA). Harder voices in Tehran pushed back: parliament deputy speaker Ali Nikzad said "The window for negotiations with our adversaries is now closed because they have nothing new to offer," and lawmaker Esmail Kowsari said Iran will not negotiate until seven conditions set by the Supreme National Security Council secretary are implemented (Iran International). For Washington, Assistant Secretary of State Donald Blome told the Manama Dialogue on 01 NOV that "the hand of negotiation has been extended, and those channels have continued to be open and active," but that the US is prepared to take a different course and will not let Iran hold the region and global economy hostage (AP via Iran International and Times of Israel). Separately, China said on 02 NOV it will send Middle East envoy Zhai Jun to Iran, Saudi Arabia, and Oman this month (China Daily; Arab News).
- US IMPACT ASSESSMENT: A draft text on the table is the first concrete sign of progress, and it gives Trump a diplomatic off-ramp right as the post-election strike window opens. But Iran still demands the blockade end first, which is the main US lever, so a deal before Wednesday remains unlikely. China's envoy trip adds a second outside mediator with a stake in Gulf oil.
- SOURCE SYNOPSIS: Reuters via Al-Monitor and Rappler (02 NOV, read directly); Reuters via CNA (02 NOV, search excerpt); AP "Where things stand" and Mideast roundup (02 NOV, search excerpts); Iran International (01 NOV, read directly); Times of Israel and Dawn (Blome, search summaries); China Daily, Global Times, Arab News (envoy, search summaries).
