Cooper's visit and the mine-clearance claim are meant to reassure shippers and markets that the strait is usable even without a deal. The highest-risk window still looks like the period after 03 NOV, but the President has kept the timing open.

## ITEM 2: Iran Diplomacy — Saudi, Pakistani, and Turkish Leaders in Tehran Monday; Iran Says Messages to the US Continue; Ghalibaf Warns of a Response "Beyond the Imagination"; Iran Warns Germany

- WHAT / WHERE / WHEN: On 26 OCT, Saudi Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman, Pakistan's army chief Field Marshal Asim Munir, and Turkish intelligence chief Ibrahim Kalin are in Tehran to meet President Pezeshkian and Supreme National Security Council secretary Mohsen Rezaei. Saudi deputy minister for public diplomacy Rayed Krimly said the visit is part of the Mecca Joint Defense Agreement states' efforts at "deescalation between parties involved in the ongoing crisis" and supports Qatar's and Pakistan's mediation (Arab News; Reuters via Al-Monitor; RFE/RL; AP). Reuters says it is the first high-level contact between Iran and all three pact members since the Houthis escalated against Saudi Arabia. SNSC deputy Ali Bagheri Kani said the meeting was Munir's proposal (Mehr). RFE/RL notes Pakistan ratified the pact last week and Turkey's parliament has not. Iran International reports Tasnim's source rejected any "Strait of Hormuz in exchange for the blockade" formula and said Iran stands by its seven conditions; Iran International notes the denial targeted Al Arabiya's report, not the ambassador's remarks to AP. Fars warned that German bases in the region would become legitimate targets if Berlin joins action against Iran, after a Hamburg court jailed two men prosecutors tied to the Quds Force and Germany summoned Iran's ambassador (X News summary).

It offers a possible channel for a deal before any US decision, but Iran's public line is still its seven conditions, with no Hormuz-for-blockade trade. A Saudi role also matters because a US strike would put Saudi territory and Gulf bases at further risk.
