## ITEM 1: Congress — House Kills Hegseth Impeachment 211–204 on Motion to Refer; Massie Lone GOP No; Trump: "EXONERATION"

Republicans voted 210–1 for referral (Massie the lone no, with 7 not voting), Democrats 0–203 (11 not voting), and one independent voted yes (Clerk). Massie said Hegseth "has violated the norms of war" and "somebody's got to put their foot down" (NBC). Outgoing Rep. Trump posted that Hegseth "gets an 'A' PLUS, PLUS, PLUS," and after the vote called it an "EXONERATION of a Great American Patriot" (NBC). Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries has said Democrats will "run Pete Hegseth out of town" when they take over next year (NBC).
- US IMPACT ASSESSMENT: Hegseth is safe through the lame duck. The fight now moves to the Democratic House in January, where oversight hearings and a renewed impeachment effort are likely, though Senate conviction remains out of reach.
