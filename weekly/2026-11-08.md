## KEY JUDGMENTS

1. A large US strike campaign on Iran remains possible but is less likely in the next two weeks than a week ago: the stated reason to wait (the election) is gone, but the vote itself weakened the political case, Vance and Trump tied the loss to the war, and the Qatar track is live. Limited "self-defense" strikes will likely continue and are the main route to escalation (MODERATE).
2. The sequencing gap is unchanged: Washington wants nuclear talks in the first round, Tehran wants blockade relief, sanctions relief, and frozen funds first. Qatar's day-one/day-two plan is the only public bridge, and Iran's added preconditions keep a signed deal unlikely this week (MODERATE-HIGH).
3. The Gulf shipping threat remains severe despite the oil selloff: JMIC kept Hormuz at SEVERE and the Gulf at SUBSTANTIAL on 08 NOV, with independent trackers showing single to low double-digit transits a day. Pump prices are falling but cannot fall far while attacks continue (HIGH).
4. The Hegseth impeachment vote is now a real event rather than a gesture: House GOP leaders told Axios they expect enough defections, Democrats are a "hard yes," and Trump says he has confidence in Hegseth. Passage in the lame-duck House is plausible; Senate removal is not (MODERATE on passage, HIGH on no conviction).

Expect early executive–congressional legal clashes before Democrats take the gavels in January, with government funding expiring 11 DEC as the first leverage point (MODERATE-HIGH).

7. Alaska and Mississippi will not resolve before about 13–25 NOV and both are likely to see legal fights; MN-1's recount does not affect House control (HIGH).
