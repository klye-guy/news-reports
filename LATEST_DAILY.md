## ITEM 2: Iran's Response and Diplomacy — Pezeshkian: "We Have to Go and Finish It"; Envoy's Hormuz-for-Blockade Offer Denied by State Media; Araghchi: Strait Management "Will No Longer Be the Same"; IRGC: Threats "for Media Consumption"; Strike on Kurdish Group in Iraq

- WHAT / WHERE / WHEN: On state TV on 24 OCT, President Pezeshkian said "these scoundrels broke the rules of the game. We were holding talks, and they disrupted them," but that on a deal "we have to go and finish it." He acknowledged that many Iranians are struggling to afford food and called for an end to the "state of neither war nor peace" (AP; CBS). Iran's ambassador to Pakistan, Reza Amiri Moghadam, told AP that Iran is open to talks under the June MOU brokered by Pakistan and Qatar and is ready to reopen Hormuz if the US lifts its blockade, and that Trump rejected this. He also said Iran will study the Mecca Joint Defense Agreement signed by Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and Turkey. Iranian state media later denied his remarks and said Iran's position remains its seven conditions (CNBC). IRGC spokesman Mohebbi said US threats are "largely for media consumption" and "Even if the enemy undertakes limited action, Iran's response will be several times greater" (Fars). On 25 OCT Foreign Minister Araghchi said, "Whether there is war or not, our policies remain the same"; that the US has "already done so twice and have seen the results"; that "Shipping routes through the Strait of Hormuz will no longer be the same, and the management of the strait will no longer be the same"; and that the region must move "towards the complete absence of foreign forces" (AFP via CBS and The Hindu). Parliament Speaker Ghalibaf urged Iranians to cut gas use by at least 10% ahead of winter (AFP via CBS), and his trip to the BRICS Parliamentary Forum in New Delhi was cancelled for "unforeseen circumstances," per the Iranian embassy (Al Jazeera). Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei met judiciary chief Ejei on 24 OCT; the readout vows to pursue "compensation for material and moral damages." He has not been seen in public since the war began (CBS). Late on 24 OCT, drones struck a base of the Iranian Kurdish group Komala in the Balisan/Alana valley northeast of Erbil, Iraq. Komala reported material damage only (Mehr; Reuters via Iran International). Reuters reporting cited by Al Jazeera says Iran has plans to target undersea Gulf internet cables. Iraq's prime minister said, "Iraq will not participate in any war."
- US IMPACT ASSESSMENT: Tehran is sending two messages: a public opening for a deal (Pezeshkian, the Pakistan channel) and a hardline insistence that it controls Hormuz (Araghchi, the IRGC). The quick denial of the ambassador's reopen-for-blockade-relief formula shows there is no settled negotiating position, which makes a diplomatic exit before any US decision harder. Iran's domestic strain, including food prices and gas rationing ahead of winter, is real pressure on the regime but has not yet produced concessions.
- SOURCE SYNOPSIS: AP and CBS (Pezeshkian, Moghadam); CNBC (state media denial); Fars (Mohebbi); AFP via CBS and The Hindu (Araghchi, Ghalibaf); Al Jazeera (BRICS trip, Reuters cable report, Iraq PM); CBS (Khamenei–Ejei readout); Mehr, Reuters via Iran International (Komala strike).
